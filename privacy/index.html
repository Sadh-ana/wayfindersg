Privacy

Privacy, in plain words

Short version: there are no accounts, no analytics and no ads. What you save stays in your browser.

What is stored on your device

Under one key, wf2.state, in your browser’s local storage: the guides you save, your try list (including any idea you type yourself), which checklist items you ticked, your display choices, and your last starting point. The older Scam Shield pages store their own progress under keys beginning wfsg. or wfl..

What is never stored

The numbers in the budget template, the cheque practice fields and the room-agreement text. They exist only while the page is open. The papers drawer stores only ticks, never document images or numbers.

What leaves your device

Nothing you type. Your browser does request fonts from Google Fonts when a page loads, so Google’s servers can see your IP address and browser details for that request. If you block those requests, the site still works with fallback fonts. Links to government and other sites take you to their pages, under their own privacy policies.

Offline copy

On https hosting the site saves a copy of its pages in your browser so it works without a connection. Clearing site data removes it.

Deleting your data

Use “Clear everything” on My Wayfinder, or clear site data in your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about this page or your data? Email sadhanasofficial40@gmail.com. There is no account to delete, because nothing about you is stored on a server.
