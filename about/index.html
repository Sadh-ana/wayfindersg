About

What this is, and what it is not

WayFinder SG is a free field guide for young people in Singapore who are moving out, starting work, or just learning how adult admin works. It covers money, paperwork, shared living, everyday health, belonging, and small things to try.

Why it exists

Growing up should not feel like being dropped in a city without a map. The aim is to make the jump from teenager to adult easier: to feel less lost, to figure out the things nobody teaches you, to stay in control of your money and your papers, to be free to try things, to be responsible for the consequences, and to have room left over to live fully.

What it is not

  • Not legal, tax, financial or medical advice. It points you to the people and pages that can give that.
  • Not an official government site, and not affiliated with any agency named in it.
  • Not a place to enter personal documents or log-in details. Never type a Singpass or banking log-in into any site you did not open yourself.

Editorial policy

  • Anything about rules, money, tax, work or health links to an official source, with the date we checked it.
  • Where there is no official source, the guide says it is general orientation and tells you whose rules apply to you.
  • Figures that change each year (tax thresholds, deadlines) are dated and labelled, and the guide tells you to read the live page.
  • Time and cost labels on ideas are rough estimates and say so.
