01 · Why this exists

Feel less lost.

Nobody sits you down the day you stop being a kid and explains the cheque, the payslip, the tax letter, the room rules. WayFinder SG exists to make that jump easier: to figure out what nobody teaches you, and to stay in control while you do it.

Less lost

A map for the first year away: what to do first, who to ask, what can wait.

What nobody teaches

Cheques, payslips, tax letters, room rules. The stuff school skipped.

In control

Your money, papers and plans. Tools that run on your device, not ours.

Free

Move, try, quit, start again. Freedom is cheaper when you know the rules.

Responsible

Pay on time, keep records, dodge scams, look after the people you live with.

Fully alive

Admin is the boring part so the rest is not. Make things, join things, try things.
