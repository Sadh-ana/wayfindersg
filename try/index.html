Small things to try

Low-pressure ideas, each with a first move you can do today. Times and costs are rough estimates, labelled as such. Nothing here is a target.

CreateEstimate: 10 minutesFree

Sketch your block from memory

Draw the walk from your door to the nearest food, with the landmarks you actually notice.

First move: Set a 10-minute timer and draw it on any paper. Fix nothing.

Access: Needs pen and paper. Can be done as a spoken description or a list of landmarks instead.

CreateEstimate: 30 minutes to startVaries (borrowing is free)

Learn three guitar chords

Three chords is enough to fake a surprising number of songs.

First move: Find a guitar to borrow, or book a beginner taster.

Access: Some chord shapes are hard with small hands or limited finger movement. Ukulele or keyboard are gentler options.

CreateEstimate: 45 minutesEstimate: under $10

Cook one dish you have never made

Pick something with five ingredients or fewer and make it start to finish.

First move: Choose the dish and check what your shared kitchen allows.

Access: Check your residence’s cooking rules first; some do not allow open cooking.

CreateEstimate: 15 minutesEstimate: about the cost of a stamp

Write a real letter to someone

Not a message. A letter, with a stamp, to someone you have not properly told thank you.

First move: Write only the first line. You can stop there.

Access: Can be an email or voice note if handwriting is hard.

CreateEstimate: 2 minutes a dayFree

Photograph doors for a week

Doors, gates, void-deck grilles. See what a neighbourhood looks like at hand height.

First move: Take one photo of a door today.

Access: Uses a phone camera. Ask before photographing people’s homes up close.

CreateEstimate: 15 minutesFree

Make a playlist for one place

Build a 10-song list that belongs to your room, a bus ride, or a rainy walk.

First move: Pick the place and add the first song.

Access: Headphone use on public transport: keep volume low enough to hear announcements.

MoveEstimate: 45 minutesFree

Walk part of a park connector

Pick a stretch you have never walked and see where it comes out.

First move: Open a map and pick a start and end point under 3 km apart.

Access: Check the path is step-free if that matters. Go in daylight and bring water.

MoveEstimate: 2 minutesFree

Take the stairs once a day for a week

One flight at a time, no targets, no tracking.

First move: Take the stairs next time you are going up three floors or fewer.

Access: Skip it on bad knee days. Lifts exist for a reason.

MoveEstimate: 1 hourEstimate: varies, often under $30

Book a taster class in something unfamiliar

Bouldering, dance, a martial art, yoga. One session, no commitment.

First move: List three options near you and read the beginner details.

Access: Ask the studio about accessibility and what to wear before booking.

MoveEstimate: 10 extra minutesFree

Walk a different way to class

Same destination, new route, ten extra minutes.

First move: Check a map for one alternative route.

Access: Pick a lit, busy route if you are walking at night.

MoveEstimate: 5 minutesFree

Do five minutes of stretching before sleep

Gentle, no equipment, no goals about flexibility.

First move: Do three slow stretches that feel good and stop.

Access: Stop anything that hurts. This is not medical advice.

MoveEstimate: 2 hoursEstimate: bike rental varies

Cycle somewhere with a friend

Pick a destination, bring snacks, go slowly.

First move: Ask one person and suggest a date, even a loose one.

Access: Check local rules on where bicycles are allowed. Helmets are sensible.

LearnEstimate: 5 minutesFree

Learn ‘hello’ in your neighbour’s language

If your hall-mates or neighbours speak other languages at home, learn one greeting from them.

First move: Ask someone how they say good morning at home.

Access: Ask rather than assume which language someone speaks.

LearnEstimate: 10 minutesFree

Look up one money word you pretend to understand

CPF, GIRO, interest, premium. Pick one and read an official page about it.

First move: Pick the word and open the MoneySense site.

Access: Text-heavy. A screen reader or read-aloud tool works fine on the official pages.

LearnEstimate: 20 minutesEstimate: under $5 for a tool

Learn to fix one small thing

A loose cabinet hinge, a bicycle chain, a button.

First move: Find a how-to video for it and watch it once.

Access: Check what repairs your residence lets you do yourself. Report anything structural.

LearnEstimate: 10 minutesFree

Learn one bus route end to end

Pick a route you have never taken. Know the stops, the first and last bus, and where you can transfer.

First move: Look up the route and screenshot the stop list.

Access: Check whether the vehicles on that route have step-free access.

LearnEstimate: 1 hourFree

Spend one hour on a topic you have no reason to know

Mushrooms, sundials, stamps, ferry timetables. Zero practical value required.

First move: Pick one topic and search for a beginner explainer.

Access: Any format. Audio or video works as well as reading.

LearnEstimate: 1 hourFree to browse; check borrowing rules

Wander a public library with no goal

Walk the shelves in a section you never visit and borrow one book on impulse.

First move: Find your nearest public library and its opening hours.

Access: Many libraries have step-free access. Check hours before going.

ConnectEstimate: 1 minuteFree

Ask one person one real question

Not ‘how are you’. Something you actually want to know, like how they got into the club they are in.

First move: Choose one person who seems approachable and ask next time you see them.

Access: Fine to do over text or in a group chat.

ConnectEstimate: 1 hourEstimate: under $15

Cook for one other person

Invite someone for a simple meal. Low effort is the point.

First move: Pick the dish and send the invitation.

Access: Ask about dietary needs and kitchen rules before you invite.

ConnectEstimate: 1 hourFree or varies

Go to one club meeting and leave early if you want

You are allowed to attend once and decide nothing.

First move: Find one open event and note the time and place.

Access: Ask the organiser about accessibility. Going with a friend is fine.

ConnectEstimate: 5 minutesFree

Make a small ritual with a hall-mate

Tea on Sundays, a shared bin run, a 10pm stretch.

First move: Suggest it once. If it does not land, let it go.

Access: Fine to do virtually if you are in different places.

ConnectEstimate: 3 hoursFree

Volunteer for a single session

One afternoon, not a commitment.

First move: Look for one single-session opportunity and read what is expected.

Access: Check the role’s physical demands and the training offered.

ConnectEstimate: 5 minutesFree

Message a friend you have lost touch with

Say one specific thing you remember about them.

First move: Open the chat and write one sentence.

Access: Voice notes work too.

CareEstimate: 5 minutesFree

Notice your room’s light at 10pm

Is it too bright, too noisy, too warm? Change one thing.

First move: Stand in the room at 10pm and note what you would change.

Access: Noise and light fixes depend on shared living agreements. See the shared-room guide.

CareEstimate: 1 minuteFree

Put a water bottle where you will see it

One tiny cue, no tracking.

First move: Fill a bottle and leave it on your desk.

Access: If you have a health condition affecting fluids, follow your doctor’s advice instead.

CareEstimate: 10 minutesFree

Write down three worries and one small step for each

Not to solve them. To get them out of your head.

First move: Write one worry and one thing that might help, however small.

Access: If worries feel overwhelming, see the support-pathways guide.

CareEstimate: 20 minutesFree

Do a 20-minute Sunday reset

Wash a few things, clear the desk, look at the week ahead.

First move: Set a 20-minute timer and stop when it rings.

Access: Adapt to your energy. Doing part of it counts.

CareEstimate: 30 minutesEstimate: cost of the meal

Eat one meal a week with someone

Eating alone all the time can wear you down. Make one meal social.

First move: Ask one person if they are eating at a time you are free.

Access: Fine to eat together silently, or on a call.

CareEstimate: 10 minutesFree

Take a slow evening walk with no phone

Ten minutes, no podcast, no photos.

First move: Leave your phone on the desk and walk around the block.

Access: Pick a lit and busy route if walking after dark. Tell someone if you feel unsafe.
