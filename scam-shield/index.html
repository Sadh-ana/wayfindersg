Practise being hard to rush

Scams are built to make you act before you think. This section lets you practise the pause on realistic messages, where being wrong costs nothing.

The pause ruleStop. Do not click, reply or pay. Check through a channel you opened yourself. Tell one person. If money or log-ins have gone, call your bank straight away.
