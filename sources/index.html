Sources

Where the facts come from

Every source used in the guides, with the date it was last checked and what we took from it. Government and institutional pages are preferred; commercial pages are labelled.

Government

  • Basic guide for new individual taxpayers · IRAS · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Read for Year of Assessment 2026 (income earned in 2025). Dates and thresholds change each year; re-check before quoting.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Tax: finding out whether you need to file, Freelancing: the first questions
  • How do I check if I need to file a tax return for YA 2026? · IRAS via Ask.gov.sg · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Names the four myTax Portal messages and the penalty line for not filing when required.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Tax: finding out whether you need to file
  • Basic guide for self-employed persons · IRAS · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Only the page summary was machine-readable when checked: a checklist on keeping accounts, preparing a statement of accounts and filing. Editors should read it in full.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Freelancing: the first questions
  • Taking stock of your finances · MoneySense (MAS) · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Source of the list-income-then-expenses method and the ‘save at least 20% of income’ checklist line.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: A first budget you can actually keep
  • Managing investment risk · MoneySense (MAS) · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Risk vs return, diversification, time horizon, ‘how much you can afford to lose’.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Investing: the words before the products
  • What is investing? · MoneySense (MAS) · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Source of the ‘build a foundation first’ list (daily expenses, emergency fund of about 3 to 6 months’ income, basic health and life insurance).
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Investing: the words before the products
  • Itemised payslips · Ministry of Manpower · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Lists what a payslip must show and when it must be given.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Reading your first payslip
  • How do I know if I am engaged under a contract of service or contract for service? · Ministry of Manpower · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Employee or self-employed ‘depends on the actual relationship’; links MOM’s self-assessment tool.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Freelancing: the first questions, Reading your first payslip
  • How to check a scam message · ScamShield · checked 19 Nov 2026
    The four outcomes the app can return, and what it submits for you.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Spotting scams, and what to do first
  • ScamShield · ScamShield (NCPC, SPF, GovTech) · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Current warning signs and reporting routes live here and change; always follow the live page.
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Spotting scams, and what to do first
  • Mental health services · Ministry of Health · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Names National mindline 1771, Samaritans of Singapore 1767, CREST-Youth (12 to 25), CHAT (16 to 30).
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Where to start when your head is loud
  • Whole-of-society efforts to support mental health and well-being of youths in Singapore · Ministry of Health · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Background on community youth mental-health services (listed in the product brief).
    Open the source (opens in a new tab) Used in: Where to start when your head is loud
  • How can I prepare for my appointment? · HealthHub support · checked 19 Nov 2026
    Appointment notes in HealthHub list what to bring and any preparation such as fasting.

  • mindline.sg · Ministry of Health · checked 19 Nov 2026

  • IRAS: Individual income tax · IRAS · checked 19 Nov 2026

  • MoneySense · MAS · checked 19 Nov 2026
