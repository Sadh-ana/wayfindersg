A first budget you can actually keep

List what comes in, list what goes out, and look at the gap. A template, not a financial plan.

What this coversGeneral education, not individual financial advice. The method follows MoneySense’s ‘take stock’ guidance. All numbers you enter stay in this browser tab and are not saved.
Do this first · 2 to 10 minutes

Open your banking app and write down three recurring payments you had forgotten about.

A budget does not have to be strict. It just has to show you where money goes. MoneySense’s method has two lists: what you earn, what you spend. Subtract the second from the first.

Costs people forget

  • Phone plan and subscriptions that renew yearly.
  • Transport top-ups, which can be small and frequent.
  • Laundry, printing, and course materials.
  • Social costs: meals out, birthday gifts, group activities.
  • Annual or semester fees, which arrive as one scary bill.
  • A small cushion for things that break.

Monthly budget template

Type monthly amounts in dollars. This is a template, not advice. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere, and it clears when you leave the page.
