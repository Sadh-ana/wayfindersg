Booking and preparing for a routine appointment

Book it, bring the right things, and know what to say. Not for emergencies.

What this coversOrientation from HealthHub and NHG Polyclinics. Booking routes and rules differ by clinic and cluster. This is not medical advice. For an emergency, call 995.
Do this first · 2 to 10 minutes

Write down your regular medication and one or two questions before you book.

ImportantIn an emergency call 995. For help that is not urgent, this guide is for you.

Booking

  • At NHG polyclinics you can book, reschedule or cancel through the NHG Health App or the Contact Centre. Same-day appointments for acute conditions can be made through the HealthHub app. NHG asks you to book ahead.
  • Dental services at NHG polyclinics are by appointment only. Registration opens 30 minutes before, and arriving more than 15 minutes late may mean rebooking. Patients under 21 need a parent or guardian to complete a consent form.
  • Walk-in waits vary with how busy the clinic is.

[2]

Before you go [1]

HealthHub says appointment details list what to bring and any preparation, such as fasting. Read the details for your appointment before you leave. [1]

  1. List your symptoms, when they started, and what makes them better or worse.
  2. List any medicines or supplements you take.
  3. Write down two questions.
  4. Bring your ID and any appointment details as instructed.
What varies, so check yoursFees, subsidies, hours and rules for dental and vaccinations. Check the clinic’s page.
