The first-week room routine

Laundry, bins, food storage and a cleaning rhythm, set up early so they stop being a daily decision.

What this coversGeneral orientation. Facilities and rules differ by residence.
Do this first · 2 to 10 minutes

Walk to the laundry area once and note how it is paid for and when it is quiet.

Most of the early stress of living away is logistics you did not know existed. A few routines, set in the first week, take most of it away.

Sort these in week one

  1. Laundry: where it is, how you pay, which times are busy. Pick a regular slot.
  2. Rubbish and recycling: where the bins are and what goes in each. Ask a neighbour if there is no sign.
  3. Food: mark your shelf, store dry food in closed containers, and do not leave cooked food out for long in the heat.
  4. Cleaning: a ten-minute reset twice a week beats a three-hour rescue once a month.
  5. Repairs: find out who to message when something breaks, and keep the contact in your phone.
