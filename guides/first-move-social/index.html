Making the first move, gently

Low-pressure ways to meet people when you move somewhere new.

What this coversGeneral orientation. No claims about particular clubs or events.
Do this first · 2 to 10 minutes

Say hello to one person you will see again this week and ask them one question.

Almost everyone in a new place is hoping someone else will start. You do not need to be charming; you need to be a little brave for ten seconds.

Three small moves

  1. Use repeat contact. People you see weekly, in the same class or corridor, are easier to know than strangers at a one-off event.
  2. Ask a real question. ‘How did you end up doing this course?’ works better than ‘what do you study?’
  3. Offer a small, specific invitation. ‘I am getting lunch at 1, want to come?’ is easier to say yes to than ‘we should hang out’.

If it does not click

Not everyone will become a friend, and that is normal. Try a different group, a different time or a smaller setting. If you feel persistently lonely, the support guide lists places to talk.

What can waitDeciding who your real friends are. That takes months.
What varies, so check yoursWhether you prefer groups, one-to-one or online first. All are fine.

Sources

This guide makes no official-rule claims, so it cites none. It is general orientation. Where rules matter (your residence, your bank, your employer), their own pages are the source.
