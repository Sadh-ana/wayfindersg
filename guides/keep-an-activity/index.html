Finding an activity you will actually keep

Pick by friction, not by ambition. A two-week trial design.

What this coversGeneral orientation. No health claims or targets are made. Check with a professional before changing your exercise if you have a health condition.
Do this first · 2 to 10 minutes

Pick one activity and list what would make it easier to do (place, time, person).

Most abandoned hobbies fail on logistics, not motivation: too far, too expensive, wrong time, nobody to go with. Lower the friction first.

A two-week trial

  1. Pick one activity, and one reason it appeals that is not about how you look.
  2. Make it easy: a place close to home or class, a time already in your week, one person to go with if you want.
  3. Do it three times. Notice how you feel afterwards, not how well you did.
  4. At the end of two weeks, choose: keep, change, or drop. Dropping counts as learning.
What can waitEquipment, memberships, perfect routines, and targets.
What varies, so check yoursCosts, availability and accessibility. Ask before you book.
