Where to start when your head is loud

A short map of who to contact, for you or a friend. It is not a diagnosis tool.

What this coversBased on the Ministry of Health’s mental health services page. Numbers and services change; confirm on the MOH page. If you are in danger now, call 995.
Do this first · 2 to 10 minutes

Save mindline 1771 in your phone now, before you need it.

ImportantIf you or someone is in immediate danger, call 995 or go to a hospital emergency department. Samaritans of Singapore is 1767, available 24 hours.

Pathways listed by MOH [1]

  • National mindline 1771: 24/7 support by call, WhatsApp (6669 1771) or online chat.
  • Click mindline.sg: self-help tools, resources and an online community.
  • CREST-Youth: community mental health teams for ages 12 to 25. Book through the provider directory on mindline.sg.
  • CHAT at the Institute of Mental Health: assessments and consultations for ages 16 to 30.

[1]

If you are not sure you need ‘help’

You do not have to be in crisis to talk to someone. If your sleep, appetite, study or friendships have been off for a few weeks, that is a good enough reason to reach out.

If it is a friend

  1. Ask them how they are, then listen without fixing.
  2. Share one of the numbers above and offer to sit with them while they call.
  3. If you think they are in danger, call 995 and stay with them.
  4. Look after yourself too.
What varies, so check yoursWaiting times, costs and referrals differ by service. Ask when you contact them.
