Small first actions

  1. A first budget you can actually keep
    Open your banking app and write down three recurring payments you had forgotten about.
  2. Reading your first payslip
    Open your latest payslip and tick the 12 items Singapore’s MOM says it should show, if they apply.
  3. Write and deposit a cheque
    Practise with the cheque lab below, then check your bank’s own guide for its exact wording.
  4. Investing: the words before the products
    Read MoneySense’s ‘What is investing?’ page and note which of the three foundations you already have.
  5. Spotting scams, and what to do first
    Open ScamShield’s ‘how to check a message’ page and read the four possible outcomes once.
